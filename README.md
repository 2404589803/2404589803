# Junfeng Xiao

AI Research Engineer · Deep Learning · Data Science

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, deep learning, and
scientific computing. I am interested in building useful machine learning
systems, understanding how they work, and turning research ideas into clear,
reproducible implementations.

## Research interests

- Deep learning and representation learning
- Generative and multimodal AI
- Model training, evaluation, and reproducible experimentation
- Data analysis and scientific computing

## Research & engineering

My everyday toolkit includes Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras,
Transformers, scikit-learn, NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, CUDA, and Jupyter. For
software engineering and infrastructure, I also work with Go, Node.js,
Docker, MySQL, Git, and VS Code.
